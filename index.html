7 insights from the full dataset

  1. Platform leadership. Netflix earns the most (₹17,641 Cr, 24.6%), then Amazon Prime Video (23.3%). Apple TV+ is smallest at 4.3%.
  2. Quality vs platform. Disney+ Hotstar has the best average IMDb rating (6.84), SonyLIV the lowest (6.60).
  3. Genre. Action earns the most (₹19,376 Cr), yet Horror returns the best ROI (179%) and Fantasy the lowest (28%).
  4. Language. Hindi is 38.9% of titles, then English (20.9%). Korean titles rate highest on average (7.08).
  5. Format. 370 movies and 280 web series. Web series average 6.86 IMDb against 6.69 for movies.
  6. Trend. 42.0% of titles released in 2023–2025, with a peak of 109 titles in 2025.
  7. Audience. U/A is the largest group (45.2%); adult (A) titles are 39.2%.

Inside the workbook

Raw Data (675 records) → Cleaned Data (650 titles, Profit and ROI % added) → Pivot Tables → Charts → Dashboard → Insights. Cleaning fixed messy text ("HORROR "), units ("174 min"), duplicates and missing ratings.
